# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html)
version 2.1 as our Code of Conduct. The full text is linked above; the summary below is
the enforceable part.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Nodal a harassment-free experience for everyone —
including newcomers, transit enthusiasts, designers, and first-time contributors changing
their first map file.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and plain-spoken; technical disagreement is fine, personal attacks are not.
- Respect trademarks and fictional-city examples in `examples/`. Do not upload
  copyrighted maps, logos, or photography.
- Flag feedback privately and specifically; assume good faith in ambiguous cases.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, trolling, discriminatory language, publishing private data, or sustained
disruption. Moderators act in the community's best interest and may remove content or
ban contributors.

## Scope

This Code applies to project spaces (repo, issues, PRs, chats) and to conduct outside
them when it is reasonably expected to affect the community.

## Enforcement

Report to the repository maintainers through a private channel (GitHub private
advisory or email listed in the repo). Reports are reviewed confidentially.
