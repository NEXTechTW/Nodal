# Brand notes

Nodal is the app. The monorepo is Linewright. Use "Nodal" in the headline.

The logo is the interchange glyph: two circles sharing a ring. It lives at `apps/web/public/logo.svg`.

How it writes: speak to the reader ("you"), start buttons with a verb (Save, Delete station), and when something fails say what to do next. No jokes in error messages. If an edit can be undone, just undo it instead of asking first.

Look and feel: one gray scale and one blue for the chrome. Line colors are for lines only, do not reuse them on buttons. Type is Noto Sans with system fallback, 14px body, 12px captions, tabular numbers for coordinates. Corners follow outer = inner + padding. Buttons press to 0.96 and respect reduced motion.

What we do not do: no real city names or logos in examples, and do not let your files very become GIS data. They are drawings.
